How 20k-scale is obtained

The IEEE morphology set is only 1,129 X-rays. Twenty-thousand scale is GRAZPEDWRI-DX (20,327 wrist trauma radiographs) plus FracAtlas (4,083) and the YOLO anatomical set (~3.7k). Combined unique estimate ≈ 28,000. That is not 20k labelled examples of every morphology class.
